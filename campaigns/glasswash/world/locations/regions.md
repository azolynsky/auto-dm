# Regions & travel times

The Bookkeeper uses this when advancing time. The skiff makes ≈ 40 miles/day with fair wind; half that threading the shallows, where someone must read the water from the bow.

| From → To | Distance | By skiff |

| Lanternwatch → Brinemarket | 35 miles, open water | 1 day |
| Lanternwatch → Sunken Vel (the old capital) | 20 miles | passable only at strong ebb |
