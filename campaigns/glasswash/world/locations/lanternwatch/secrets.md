# Lanternwatch — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Hidden facts about the place

- The great lamp's glass-ember is a piece of Vel — and it is why the shallows near Lanternwatch behave. The wild-magic patches on the charts map where the empire's other embers lie. The Collegium's founders knew this; the current magisters have half-forgotten it into ritual.
- The chartroom's oldest master chart shows the Reach *before* the drowning, and it is kept under lock not because it is precious but because the coastline on it is wrong in a way that upsets people who stare too long.
- The 111-year tide that raised the new spire is on that old chart, in the drowned empire's own notation. Someone at the Collegium can read it. Decide who when it matters.

## Hidden factions / agents present

- One salvage crew, the *Cormorant*'s, takes quiet retainers from a mainland buyer who wants Vel-glass in quantity and doesn't ask what wakes when it's moved. Rivals first, antagonists if fed.

## Hidden connections to other locations

- The whale songlines pass directly over Sunken Vel — the singers are answering something. See the Songlines hook.
