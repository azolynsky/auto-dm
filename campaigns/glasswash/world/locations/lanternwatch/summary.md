# Lanternwatch

A lighthouse grown into a college: the great lamp on top, then five floors of library, chartroom, and workshops spiraling down the basalt stack to a harbor of skiffs and salvage barges. The Lantern Collegium keeps the light, keeps the charts, and pays for both to be improved.

## At a glance
- **Population**: ~250 — magisters, students, keepers, and crews between charters
- **Government**: the Collegium's chartmasters; Magister [[campaigns/glasswash/npcs/recurring/ilyen-voss/summary|Ilyen Voss]] runs the charter desk
- **Economy**: charters, chart-copying, salvage assay; the light itself is funded by every port on the Reach
- **Threat level**: low on the stack; the Reach beyond is exactly as dangerous as it is interesting
- **Mood**: scholarly bustle over deep respect for the water

## Notable features / buildings
- **The Chartroom** — the Reach's master charts, corrected daily; charters are posted and paid here
- **The great lamp** — burns a salvaged glass-ember from Vel; never goes out (see secrets)
- **The harbor stair** — 212 steps, and everyone counts them aloud

## Active factions present
- **The Lantern Collegium** — scholars and keepers; the party's patron
- **Freelance salvage crews** — friendly rivals; the Ninth Tide will not stay friendly
