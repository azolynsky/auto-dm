# Setting overview

**The Glasswash Reach**: a hundred miles of turquoise shallows, coral-grown ruin, and small green islands — all of it sitting on top of a drowned empire whose glass towers still break the surface at low tide. The living center is **[[campaigns/glasswash/world/locations/lanternwatch/summary|Lanternwatch]]**, a lighthouse-college on a basalt stack.

## The region in one paragraph

The empire of Vel built in glass and sank in a single tide, long enough ago that nobody grieves and recently enough that the towers still hold air. The sea over it never quite behaves: compasses wander, tides keep two calendars, and magic runs strong in patches the charts mark with a spiral. The Lantern Collegium pays standing rates for soundings, rubbings, and wonders — which makes a small crew with a sail-skiff and nerve exactly as important as it sounds. Trouble here is mostly *found* rather than suffered: you sail toward it, on purpose, with blank charts aboard.

## Major settlements

- **Lanternwatch** — the lighthouse-college, pop. ~250. Chartroom, library, harbor. **This is where the party starts.**
- **Brinemarket** — a floating bazaar lashed together from hulls, a day's sail east. Referenced, not detailed.

## Tone targets

*(Set during session 0. Default: bright wonder with deep water under it — the Reach is generous to the curious and hard on the greedy. Combat exists; it is rarely the interesting answer.)*
