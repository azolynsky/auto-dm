# Ilyen Voss

**Race / age**: half-elf, apparent 50s
**Role**: chartmaster; runs the Collegium's charter desk and signs its coin
**Disposition (public)**: dreamy until the details start, then exact; collects impossible things and first questions
**Voice (short)**: trails off mid-sentence when an idea lands; always asks "and what did it *smell* like?" *(full samples in voice.md)*
**Appearance**: tall, salt-grey queue, coat with too many pockets, each holding one strange small thing; ink on the left hand always

## Family / Key relationships
- The Collegium is the family; feuds gently with two other chartmasters over shelf space
- Keeps a locked drawer of things brought back that should not exist; the party will add to it

## Status
Alive. Last seen: in the Chartroom at dawn, weighting the new spire's charter with a whale's tooth.
