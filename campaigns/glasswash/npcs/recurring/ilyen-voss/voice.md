# Ilyen Voss — voice

**Accent**: educated, unhurried, faintly amused
**Verbal tics**: trails off when thinking ("…mm. Yes."); asks for sensory detail no one expects to be asked for; says "the sea keeps two calendars" the way other people say "weather permitting"
**Cadence**: long looping sentences that land, suddenly, on an exact number
**Register** (varies by audience):
- With new crews: courteous, testing whether they *notice* things
- With trusted crews: conspiratorial, shares the strange drawer
- Under stress: goes very quiet and very precise

## Canonical lines

> "Open water on every chart we own — and now, a tower. I find I would like to know its opinion on the matter."
> "Bring me one true thing. A rubbing will do. A story will *not* — stories float; facts sink and stay put."
> "Fifty gold, the skiff, and my attention. The last is worth the most, but I understand you can't eat it."
> "If the glass shows you a door, note the hinges before you note the door. Hinges tell you who expected to come back."

## Things they do NOT do
- Doesn't rush a report — will make the party sit, eat, and tell it properly
- Doesn't buy salvage torn out for coin's sake; the Collegium pays for knowledge, not loot
- Doesn't laugh at any account, however strange — writes it down
