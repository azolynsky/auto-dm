# Setting overview

**The High Saddle**: a glacier-carved pass through the Ironteeth range, walkable four months a year and worth a war the other eight. Its keystone is **[[campaigns/kraghold/world/locations/kraghold/summary|Kraghold]]**, a dwarven road-fort that has never fallen — a record currently under review.

## The region in one paragraph

This is siege country: one fort, one pass, one warband that wants through, and winter holding everyone's coat. The Red Howl — ogres, wolf-riders, and worse — corked the pass three weeks ago and probe the walls nightly. Caravans shelter in Kraghold's yard, the granary is counted twice a day, and the pay for sword-hands has never been better. Trouble here is not small and it is not personal; it comes over the wall in numbers.

## Major settlements

- **Kraghold** — the road-fort, ~300 souls plus stranded caravans. **This is where the party starts.**
- **Thaneway** — the valley town a week below the pass. Relief could come from there. It hasn't.
