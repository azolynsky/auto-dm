# Kraghold

A road-fort of grey ashlar wedged across the narrowest point of the High Saddle, built by dwarves who measured in centuries. One gate, one yard crowded with stranded caravans, and the **Long Wall** — two hundred paces of battlement facing the scree slope the Red Howl probe every night.

## At a glance
- **Population**: ~300 garrison and hold-folk, plus ~80 stranded caravaners
- **Government**: Captain [[campaigns/kraghold/npcs/recurring/brunna-stonehewn/summary|Brunna Stonehewn]], by right of being the one everyone obeys
- **Economy**: road tolls in peacetime; right now, siege arithmetic — the granary is counted in weeks
- **Threat level**: high and nightly
- **Mood**: grim, profane, unbroken; the wall-watch sings on the bad nights

## Notable features / buildings
- **The Long Wall** — west-facing battlement; the party's post
- **The armory** — better stocked than the granary; Brunna opens it for those who earn it
- **The Deep Door** — a sealed gate in the cellar, dwarf-work older than the fort above it, lately thudding at night
- **The yard** — caravan wagons circled like a town square

## Active factions present
- **Kraghold's garrison** — outnumbered, professional
- **The Red Howl** (outside) — ogre-led warband; wolf-riders, ladder-builders, and shamans who find paths that shouldn't exist
