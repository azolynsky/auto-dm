# Kraghold — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Hidden facts about the place

- The scree is trackless because the raiders move under it: goat tunnels riddle the slope, found by the Red Howl's shamans. Several connect to the same under-road the **Deep Door** seals. The wall was never the weak point; the cellar is.
- The Deep Door's thudding is not the Red Howl. It is something fleeing along the under-road *toward* the surface — refugees of the deep kingdom, or the first tremor of the wyrm. Choose when the door matters.
- The granary count Brunna announces is two weeks kinder than the truth.

## Hidden factions / agents present

- **The Red Howl** are fleeing, not conquering: a frost wyrm waking deeper in the Ironteeth has burned them out of their valleys. Their war-leader would trade the siege for a way through — or a way to kill it. This is the campaign's hinge: the enemy has the map the defenders need.

## Hidden connections to other locations

- The under-road runs to the sealed dwarf kingdom — and under the Red Howl's lost valleys. Every faction's road home is the same tunnel.
