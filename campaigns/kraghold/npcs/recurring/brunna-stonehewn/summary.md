# Brunna Stonehewn

**Race / age**: dwarf, 140s
**Role**: captain of Kraghold's garrison; paymaster of every blade in the hold
**Disposition (public)**: gruff, fair, spends lives like her own coin — reluctantly and at full price
**Voice (short)**: parade-ground volume indoors; counts everything in shields *(full samples in voice.md)*
**Appearance**: barrel-built, iron-grey braids wound with wire, a notch in her jaw she doesn't explain, wall-hammer on her back

## Family / Key relationships
- Third Stonehewn to hold the wall; her grandmother's name is carved over the gate
- Argues nightly with the granary-keeper, who is also her wife

## Status
Alive. Last seen: on the west bastion at dusk, assigning posts for the night watch.
