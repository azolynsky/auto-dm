# Brunna Stonehewn — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Core truths
- The granary numbers she announces are two weeks kinder than the truth. She's rationing hope, not just grain.
- She knows the Deep Door's thudding started the same week the Red Howl arrived, and the coincidence keeps her up. She has the only key, and orders posted to shoot anyone who touches the seals.
- She would parley with the Red Howl if they offered — she's read enough sieges to know a fleeing enemy from a hungry one — but she will not offer first with the wall's morale this thin.

## Current wants
1. The wall held until thaw, at the smallest butcher's bill.
2. To know what's on the other side of the Deep Door before it comes through.
3. Hires she can promote to trusted — the garrison needs officers.

## Soft red lines
- Won't sacrifice caravaners to save garrison, or the reverse.
- Won't abandon Kraghold. If it falls, she falls on the gate stairs.
