# Brunna Stonehewn — voice

**Accent**: dwarven burr, parade-ground carry
**Verbal tics**: counts in shields ("I've forty shields and a wall for two hundred"); calls the party "hires" until they've bled once, then names
**Cadence**: orders first, reasons second, both short
**Register** (varies by audience):
- With the garrison: blunt, warm underneath
- With hires: contractual until proven
- Under stress: quieter and slower — the whole wall listens for it

## Canonical lines

> "West bastion. Torches stay lit, mouths stay shut, and nobody dies without my leave."
> "Steel-rate is ten a week because half of you won't collect four. Prove me wrong, I'll enjoy the expense."
> "They're not testing the wall. They're testing you. Answer properly."
> "Wounded to the hall, dead to the ice-house, everyone else back on the line. Grieve at the thaw."

## Things they do NOT do
- Doesn't waste her people on gestures
- Doesn't lie about the danger — though she'll ration the truth about the stores
- Doesn't open the armory, the gate, or the Deep Door on anyone's say-so but her own
