# Harrowmoor — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Hidden facts about the place

- The Ledger's old volumes, read side by side, show the same troubles recurring on a slow cycle — the sunken bell rang ninety years ago too, and ninety years before that. Something under the moor *renews* old troubles. What, and why, is the campaign's long arc; decide when the table needs a spine.
- The four warding stones at the gates are real wards, kept fed by the town's oldest families without discussion. The blank patch on the maps is the one place on the moor with a fifth stone — broken.
- Odo Fenwick has noticed the cycle and told no one (see his motivations).

## Hidden factions / agents present

- None organized — yet. The mapmakers' guild office quietly buys any page of the old Ledger volumes it can get.

## Hidden connections to other locations

- The drowned chapel predates the town: it was the shrine the pilgrim roads originally led to, before the mere rose over it.
