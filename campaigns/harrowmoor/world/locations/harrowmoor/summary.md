# Harrowmoor

A walled crossroads town of slate roofs and peat smoke, built where the moor's four old roads meet on the only dry hill for miles. Its heart is **Bellwether Hall**, where the town's troubles are posted in a great iron-bound book — the Ledger — and struck out, for coin, by whoever proves they've settled them.

## At a glance
- **Population**: ~600
- **Government**: a town moot chaired by the Keeper of the Ledger ([[campaigns/harrowmoor/npcs/recurring/odo-fenwick/summary|Odo Fenwick]])
- **Economy**: wool, peat, and — bluntly — bounties; the Ledger draws coin and trouble-solvers from three counties
- **Threat level**: low inside the walls; the moor is another matter
- **Mood**: businesslike about the uncanny — hauntings are a line item

## Notable features / buildings
- **Bellwether Hall** — moot hall; the Ledger is read aloud each morning at the bell
- **The Pilgrim's Rest** — inn by the north gate; where trouble-solvers compare postings
- **The four roads** — pilgrim ways east, west, north, south; each with a warding stone at the town wall

## Active factions present
- The mapmakers' guild keeps a small office (see the Cartographer's Gap posting). No larger faction bothers with the moor — officially.
