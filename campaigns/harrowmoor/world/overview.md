# Setting overview

**The Harrowmoor**: a cold upland of fog, standing stones, and black meres, crossed by old pilgrim roads that mostly lead somewhere worth avoiding. At its one dry crossroads sits the town of **[[campaigns/harrowmoor/world/locations/harrowmoor/summary|Harrowmoor]]**, which survives by solving the moor's troubles for coin.

## The region in one paragraph

The moor makes trouble the way other country makes weather — small hauntings, lost roads, things in the water — and the town long ago stopped pretending otherwise. Bellwether Hall keeps **the Ledger**: any citizen may post a trouble with coin behind it, and any hand may strike a posting by settling it. Adventurers are not exotic here; they are infrastructure. This world runs on picking jobs off the board, and the board never empties.

## Major settlements

- **Harrowmoor** — the crossroads town, pop. ~600. Bellwether Hall, the Ledger, the Pilgrim's Rest inn. **This is where the party starts.**
- **Loomhollow, Fernby, Stane** — moor villages a day or two out. Referenced in postings, detailed when visited.

## Tone targets

*(Set during session 0. Default: cozy-eerie — mists and mysteries with warm firelight to come home to. Most postings have a twist; few have a villain.)*
