# Odo Fenwick — voice

**Accent**: clipped town-clerk precision
**Verbal tics**: cites posting numbers from memory; corrects rounding errors mid-sentence
**Cadence**: short entries, like ledger lines; pauses to dab the pen that isn't in his hand
**Register** (varies by audience):
- With strangers: procedural — terms first, sympathy after
- With proven trouble-solvers: warmer, saves the good postings for them
- Under stress: recites the Ledger's rules like a litany

## Canonical lines

> "Posting two-fourteen. Read it twice; the mere only lets you be wrong once."
> "The town pays on proof, not on stories. Bring me something the causeway can't argue with."
> "Thirty gold. I know. If it were easy, love, it would be twelve."
> "Strike it clean and I'll have work for you by market day. The moor is generous that way."

## Things they do NOT do
- Doesn't haggle after terms are set
- Doesn't send anyone out without naming the danger as he knows it
- Doesn't gossip about who posted what — the Ledger is discreet
