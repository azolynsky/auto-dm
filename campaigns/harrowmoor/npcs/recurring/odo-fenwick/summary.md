# Odo Fenwick

**Race / age**: human, 60s
**Role**: Keeper of the Ledger; chairs the town moot; de facto quartermaster of every adventure the moor produces
**Disposition (public)**: fussy, precise, unexpectedly kind in the clutch
**Voice (short)**: refers to troubles by posting number; dry as old paper *(full samples in voice.md)*
**Appearance**: small, ink-stained fingers, spectacles on a chain, a different scarf every day

## Family / Key relationships
- Never married; the Ledger is the marriage
- Trades quiet favors with the Pilgrim's Rest's cook for first news off the roads

## Status
Alive. Last seen: at his lectern in Bellwether Hall, straightening the morning's postings.
