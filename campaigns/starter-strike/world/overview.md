# Setting overview

A deliberately small, IP-free starting canvas: **Meridian City**, a
mid-sized coastal city with room for a new super-team to make a name. Swap in
your own city, join an existing shared universe, or bring in the ready-made
X-Men-style roster (`roster/strike/characters/`, `roster/strike/villains/`)
during session 0. Nothing here is load-bearing.

## The city in one paragraph

Meridian City is big enough to have real stakes — a harbor, a downtown core,
a university district, sprawling suburbs — but not so big it needs a wiki to
track. Supers are known but not common; the public is curious more than
afraid, for now. The team's home base is **the Aerie**, a converted
rooftop comms hub with a skyline view and a handler who keeps it running.

## Major locations

- **The Aerie** — the team's HQ. A converted rooftop facility: ops floor, comms, a place to patch up after a fight. **This is where the party starts.**
- **Port Chandler** — a smaller port city up the coast, referenced, not detailed.
