Auto-DM

A Dungeons & Dragons game master that runs your table. One thing to set up, then you can play.

1 · Connect the DM's brain

Auto-DM thinks using OpenRouter — you pay them directly for what you use, usually a few cents a session. Make a free account, add a little credit, then create a key and paste it below.

Open openrouter.ai/keys →

Stored only on this computer, in this app's settings file.

2 · Name your campaign

You'll start in the village of Emberwick. Your DM can change anything once you're playing — just ask.

3 · Choose your heroes

Pick one ready-made hero per player and add each player's name. You can also make your own characters from scratch later — just tell the DM.

4 · Pick the DM

Smarter models run better games and cost a bit more. You can change this later.
