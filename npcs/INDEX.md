Maintained by the Bookkeeper. One line per NPC, alphabetical within each section. Loaded as a discovery aid — the LLM uses this to know what folders exist before reading any of them.

When a one-shot NPC reappears, the Bookkeeper promotes them: copy `recurring/_TEMPLATE/` to `recurring/<their-id>/`, move the one-shot content into `summary.md`, delete the one-shot file, add a line here.
