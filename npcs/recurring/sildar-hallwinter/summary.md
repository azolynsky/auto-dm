# Sildar Hallwinter

**Race / age**: Human, mid-40s
**Role**: Lords' Alliance operative; former soldier; hired by Gundren Rockseeker as escort/bodyguard
**Disposition (public)**: Direct, calm, professionally warm. Carries himself with quiet authority.
**Voice (short)**: Soldier's cadence — short sentences, no wasted words. Dry humor surfaces under stress.
**Appearance**: Mid-height, broad-shouldered, going slightly soft around the middle. Brown hair shot through with gray. One eye currently swollen shut (Cragmaw, Session 1). Moves like a man who has been in fights and expects more.

## Status
Rescued from Cragmaw Hideout, Session 1. Recovering at Stonehill Inn, Phandalin. Unarmed.
Currently: bruised, dehydrated, functional.

## Key relationships
- **Gundren Rockseeker**: employer and friend; Sildar is motivated to rescue him
- **Relthus & Mira**: owes them his life; considers them trustworthy assets
- **Lords' Alliance**: his actual principal; has standing orders regarding Wave Echo Cave

## What the party knows
- He's a Lords' Alliance agent, not just a hired sword
- His mission: help Gundren reach Wave Echo Cave and secure it for the Alliance
- He confirmed the Black Spider is likely a drow and arranged the ambush
- Gundren is at Cragmaw Castle; the map went with him
