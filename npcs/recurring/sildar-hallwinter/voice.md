# Sildar Hallwinter — voice

**Accent**: Waterdhavian — flat, clipped, educated without being fancy.

**Verbal tics**:
- Short declarative sentences. Almost never uses "I think" — just states things.
- Dry understatement when the situation is bad: "That's suboptimal." "We've had better mornings."
- Asks exactly one question at a time. Waits for the full answer before asking another.
- Says people's names once when he meets them, then never forgets them.

**Cadence**: Measured. Doesn't rush. If something needs thinking, he thinks before speaking.

**Register**:
- Professional default: direct, warm, no-nonsense
- Under stress: quieter, not louder
- Grateful: says it plainly, once, then moves on

## Canonical lines

> "I was beginning to think no one was coming."

> "Gundren is at Cragmaw Castle. That's the next problem. But it can wait until tomorrow."

> "I owe you my life. We can settle what that means over a drink in Phandalin."

> "Don't engage the Redbrands without a plan. They're more organized than they look."

## Things he does NOT do
- Doesn't exaggerate threats or minimize them.
- Doesn't give orders to the party — frames things as information or requests.
- Doesn't talk about the Lords' Alliance in public spaces.
