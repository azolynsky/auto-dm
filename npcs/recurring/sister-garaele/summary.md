# Sister Garaele

**Race / age**: Moon elf, ~140 (looks early 30s by human standards)
**Role**: Priestess of Tymora at the small Shrine of Luck in Phandalin
**Disposition (public)**: Calm, observant, slightly aloof; warmer with regulars
**Voice (short)**: Measured, soft, lightly accented Elvish — clipped consonants, lingering vowels
**Appearance**: Tall, slim, silver-white hair worn in a long braid. Pale gray-blue eyes. Wears Tymora's coin amulet over modest gray-blue robes.

## Public role
Tends the small stone shrine on the east edge of Phandalin. Performs blessings for travelers, simple healing for locals, funeral rites. Known to bargain on price for those who can't pay.

## Key relationships
- Quiet ally: [Toblen Stonehill](../toblen-stonehill/summary.md) — Harper contact, knows about Bremmen
- Friend: Daran Edermath — fellow Harper (formal), retired adventurer
- Watching: Halia Thornton (Miner's Exchange) — suspects Zhentarim
- Watching: [Redbrand Ruffians](../../../factions/redbrands/summary.md) — knows their leader is a wizard; doesn't yet know who

## Status
Alive. Last seen at the Shrine of Luck, midmorning prayers.
