> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Core truths

- **Active Harper agent**, formally inducted in 1476 DR. Reports to a contact in Yartar twice a year (carrier pigeons via a Triboar relay).
- **Came to Phandalin in 1487 DR** specifically because Harper intelligence flagged Tresendar Manor as a likely magical-staff-cache from a Phalorm-era survivor. She is still looking; she has not found it.
- **Knows Toblen Stonehill is a soft asset** but not a member. She values him for honest observations and would never burn him.
- **Suspects Halia Thornton is Zhentarim** based on a coded letter she intercepted in 1490; can't prove it.
- **Currently searching for Bowgentle's spellbook** — Harper standing assignment; rumored to be in a banshee's lair in Conyberry. Will offer this as a quest to any adventurer who seems competent.

## Current wants
1. Bowgentle's spellbook recovered
2. The Redbrand wizard identified and removed
3. Halia exposed or confirmed harmless (either; she just wants to know)
4. The party (when they arrive) gently steered toward Harper-aligned goals without being recruited (yet)
