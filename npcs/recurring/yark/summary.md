# Yark

**Race / age**: Goblin, young adult
**Role**: Party companion and guide. Former Cragmaw hideout guard ("GoblinBlade", captured then released Session 1).
**Disposition (public)**: Jumpy, chatty, eager to please his new "tribe." Genuinely loyal so far — the party spared him twice.
**Voice (short)**: Refers to himself in third person ("Yark thinks..."). Short bursts, repeats words for emphasis ("No. No no."). Calls the PCs "the tall ones."
**Appearance**: Small even for a goblin; one ear notched. Carries a squirrel-roasting stick like a treasured weapon.

## Status
Joined the party Session 2 in exchange for food and protection from Klarg, who blames him for the hideout losses. Guiding them to Cragmaw Castle (~2 days north through Neverwinter Wood; he's been there twice as a porter and knows paths and guard habits).

## What he knows
- The route to Cragmaw Castle, its supply entrance, and rough guard habits
- Nothing about Wave Echo Cave ("boss business — King Grol business, Spider business")
- Klarg wants his ears in a bag

## Combat
Goblin stat block (AC 15, HP 7, scimitar +4, 1d6+2). Fights only if cornered — his instinct is to hide.
