# Session 03 — The Quiet Kitchen

**Real date**: 2026-07-21
**In-game**: 3rd of Mirtul, dusk → early night
**Location at start**: Hollow beneath a rock shelf, ~half mile south of Cragmaw Castle
**Location at end**: Cragmaw Castle — kitchen (secured)

## Recap

Short session. Both PCs applied level 2 (Relthus: spellcasting — Cure Wounds, Ensnaring Strike; Mira: Action Surge). Grib was interrogated under threat and gave up the castle's layout, then Relthus executed him and burned the body — the castle didn't notice. The party scouted from the treeline, identified the messenger's giant lizard mount (drow, packed to leave), sniped the drowsy sentry on the collapsed SE wall, and slipped inside via the supply corridor. Yark laid out a back-passage route to Grol's tower avoiding the great hall (past the owlbear tower and the grick shrine). The session ended after a flawless silent takedown of the two kitchen cooks — no alarm, feast ongoing next door.
