## Log

*(Running log — updated turn by turn)*

### Opening
Party: Relthus (Wood Elf Ranger 1, 8/12 HP, 200 XP) and Mira (Human Fighter 1, 12/12 HP, hit die spent, 200 XP). Resuming at the Stonehill Inn, midday, directly after the return from Cragmaw Hideout. Sildar Hallwinter recovering at the inn.

### At the Stonehill Inn (midday)
- Party asked Sildar about the Black Spider. He shared what he knows: never seen, a drow, hires goblins, wants Gundren's map to Wave Echo Cave and the Forge of Spells. No new secrets revealed.
- Pressed further, Sildar shared: Gundren's brothers Nundro and Tharden are camped near the Wave Echo Cave entrance, unaware they may be hunted.
- Party asked about reaching the cave directly. Sildar: cave location unknown to all — only Gundren/the map know. Path runs through Cragmaw Castle. Sildar needs ~1 day to learn the castle's location via contacts; alternatively a goblin (e.g. Yark) could point the way.

### Tracking Yark (afternoon)
- Party left Phandalin north to find Yark; Sildar stayed to work contacts on the castle location.
- Relthus tracked (Survival +4, advantage via Favored Enemy, DC 12): rolled 19 — success by 7.
- Found Yark alone at a camp in a rocky gully northeast of the hideout (he's avoiding Klarg). Late afternoon.
- Approached openly; Yark recognized them (startled but friendly-scared). He's exiled — Klarg blames him for the hideout losses.
- Asked about Wave Echo Cave: Yark knows nothing of it ("boss business — King Grol business, Spider business").
- Yark's offer: he's been to Cragmaw Castle twice as a porter and knows the paths/guard habits. Will guide the party in exchange for joining them — food and protection from Klarg. Awaiting party's answer.
- Party accepted — Yark joined as companion/guide (promoted to npcs/recurring/yark). Castle is ~2 days north through Neverwinter Wood. Party camps at Yark's gully for the night.

### Night ambush at the gully camp (pre-dawn, 2nd Mirtul)
- Long rest applied first (attack came pre-dawn): Relthus back to 12/12, Mira regained her hit die.
- Night encounter roll: 1 on d100 (25% chance) — two of Klarg's goblin hunters, tracking Yark.
- Goblin group stealth 23 vs passive 14 — party surprised. Surprise round: both goblin arrows missed (9 vs Relthus AC 14, 7 vs Yark AC 15).
- Round 2: Relthus hit GoblinHunter1 (22, 4 dmg → 3/7). GoblinHunter2 missed Relthus (10). Yark killed GoblinHunter2 (19 to hit, 7 dmg). GoblinHunter1 fled.
- Rounds 3–4: Relthus chased; missed (14), then hit at disadvantage in darkness (17, 6 dmg) — GoblinHunter1 dead. No goblin escaped to report.
- No PC damage taken. Mira comforted Yark; his loyalty deepened ("GOOD tribe").
- XP: 2 goblins = 100 XP → +50 each. **Relthus and Mira now 250/300.**
- Time advanced to pre-dawn, 2nd of Mirtul. Combat ~5 min elapsed incl. aftermath.
