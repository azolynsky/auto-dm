# The Redbrand Ruffians

A roving gang of bandits and thugs that took up residence in Phandalin about a year ago and turned it into a quiet protection racket. Named for the red cloaks they wear; openly armed; openly menacing.

## Numbers
~10–15 active members at any time, plus a leadership figure ("Glasstaff") who rarely appears publicly. Membership rotates as some leave and others arrive.

## Apparent goals
- Extort coin from Phandalin merchants
- Intimidate anyone who looks like a threat
- Keep adventurers and travelers from settling in or staying long

## Base
The ruined east wing of [Tresendar Manor](../../world/locations/phandalin/summary.md), on the east edge of town.

## Known leaders
- **Glasstaff** — a wizard, identity unknown to the public. Carries a glass staff (Staff of Defense). Operates from the manor.

## Known members
- Several thuggish humans in red cloaks, no individual names yet established. *(Promote any named member to `npcs/recurring/` when they become recurring.)*

## Public reputation
Loathed but tolerated. The Townmaster won't act; the merchants pay; locals avoid the inn after dark. Trilena Stonehill once chased one out with a soup ladle. He has not returned.
