# Redbrands — members

## Leadership
- **Glasstaff** (Iarno Albrek) — wizard, hidden identity. See faction summary and `motivations.md`.

## Lieutenants
- **A bugbear named Mosk** lives in the basement and handles "discipline." Sleeps with two wolves chained.

## Rank-and-file
~10–12 human thugs in red cloaks. Stats: bandit (MM 343), or thug (MM 350) for slightly tougher ones. Light armor, shortswords, sometimes shortbows. They rotate; new ones recruited from Luskan periodically.

## Recurring promotion
When a specific Redbrand is named in play and recurs, copy `npcs/recurring/_TEMPLATE/` to `npcs/recurring/<their-id>/` and link from here.

## Captives
- **Three commoners** chained in a cell in the manor cellar (Mirna Dendrar, a Phandalin widow, and her two children). Iarno took them in retaliation for her late husband having helped Sister Garaele with something months ago. Iarno doesn't remember what; Mirna doesn't know either. *(See `secrets.md` of Phandalin for the missing detail.)*
