# Lore

Persistent setting facts the party may discover. Add to this rather than re-invent — once a fact is in here, the Continuity Checker uses it as source of truth.

## Ancient history (broad strokes)

- **Netheril** — flying-city empire, fell ~1700 years ago. Magical ruins all across the North.
- **Phalorm** — a kingdom of elves, dwarves, gnomes, and humans that thrived in the Sword Mountains. Collapsed under orcish invasions ~1400 years ago. Some dwarven holds and elven sites remain hidden.
- **Wave Echo Cave** — a lost Phalorm-era mine, ruled jointly by humans, dwarves, and gnomes. Site of the **Forge of Spells**, a magical forge where craftsmen of three races worked together. Destroyed in the orcish wars; location forgotten for centuries. **Currently sought by the Rockseeker brothers (Gundren, Tharden, Nundro).**

## Recent history (last few decades)

- **1451 DR** — Mount Hotenow erupted, devastating Neverwinter and the surrounding countryside. The Old Phandalin was destroyed in the same century by orcs from Uruth Ukrypt; the New Phandalin is the same site repopulated.
- **1480s–1490s** — Lord Neverember rebuilds Neverwinter. Frontier trade resurges; Phandalin is reborn from this.

## Gods (those most relevant)

- **Tymora** — Lady Luck. Patron of adventurers. Shrine in Phandalin.
- **Oghma** — knowledge.
- **Kelemvor** — judge of the dead.
- **Tempus** — war.
- **Asmodeus, Tiamat, Bane, Bhaal** — common evil pantheon.

## Languages (relevant)

Common, Elvish, Dwarvish, Halfling, Gnomish, Orc, Goblin, Giant, Draconic, Undercommon, plus the planar tongues (Infernal, Celestial, Abyssal, Sylvan, Primordial).

## Currency

- 1 platinum (pp) = 10 gold (gp)
- 1 gp = 10 silver (sp)
- 1 sp = 10 copper (cp)
- 1 ep (electrum) = 5 sp — rare, mostly old hoards
