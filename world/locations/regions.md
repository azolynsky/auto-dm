# Regions

Lightweight regional notes. Detail individual locations in their own folders under `world/locations/<id>/` as the party encounters them. See `world/locations/INDEX.md`.

## Sword Mountains foothills (Phandalin)

Rolling pine and scrub, broken stone outcrops. Old Netherese ruins peek out of hills. Roads are dirt tracks. Bandits, orcs, and goblins live in the surrounding caves.

Travel times:
- Neverwinter → Phandalin: ~3 days by wagon along the Triboar Trail (sometimes 4 with weather)
- Phandalin → Old Owl Well: ~1 day east
- Phandalin → Cragmaw Castle: ~1 day north-northeast

## The High Road

Coastal highway from Waterdeep north through Neverwinter to Luskan. Patrolled in the south, scarcely in the north. Carriages and merchant trains daily.

## The Mere of Dead Men

Coastal swamp south of Neverwinter. Lizardfolk, will-o'-wisps, and worse. Avoided by travelers.

## Neverwinter Wood

Old growth east of Neverwinter. Druidic territory; the Emerald Enclave maintains presence. Strange ruins (Gnomengarde, the Conyberry homestead) hidden inside.
