# Phandalin — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Hidden facts about the place

- **Tresendar Manor cellars** connect to a Phalorm-era dungeon network that the Redbrand wizard ("Glasstaff" / Iarno Albrek) is currently using as a base. The east wing collapsed in the orcish raid and was never excavated by the town. Locals know it's there; they don't know it's occupied.
- **A secret tunnel** runs from a hidden hollow tree about 100 yards east of town into the Redbrand hideout. Carp Alderleaf has been there and is one of the only people in town who knows.
- **Halia Thornton is a Zhentarim agent.** She's been positioning the Miner's Exchange to claim Wave Echo Cave's mining rights when it's rediscovered. She does not yet know about Glasstaff's identity; if she finds out, she'll try to recruit or eliminate him.
- **Wave Echo Cave** — the long-lost Phalorm mine and Forge of Spells — lies roughly a day east of Phandalin in the foothills. Its entrance is hidden behind a rockfall. Glasstaff is looking for it on behalf of the Black Spider (a drow agent of Lolth's faction). Halia is looking for it for the Zhentarim. The Rockseeker brothers found it but Gundren was captured before he could lead anyone there.
- **Beneath the Stonehill Inn cellar**, the original Phalorm-era stonework is visible if you know to look. Toblen has noticed but not investigated. Garaele has investigated quietly — no spellbook cache, to her disappointment.

## Hidden facts about who's watching whom

- **Sister Garaele watches Halia.** Halia does not know.
- **Toblen watches Halia in his own way** (won't drink her wine). She has noted his coolness but ascribes it to provincialism.
- **Glasstaff watches the trail from Neverwinter** via two Redbrand scouts. He'll know within hours when the party arrives in town.
