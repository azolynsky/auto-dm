# Phandalin — current events

What's in the air right now (campaign start).

## On everyone's mind

- **Redbrand extortion.** Has been going on for months. Townmaster won't act. People are scared. Children stopped playing in the streets after dark a month ago.
- **Goblin raids on outlying farms.** Two raids in the last six weeks. Nobody killed yet, but livestock lost and one barn burned.
- **Gundren Rockseeker is in Neverwinter** loudly hiring caravan guards for a Phandalin run. Locals know him — he and his brothers came through town a couple of weeks ago looking at properties.

## In conversation at the Stonehill Inn

- Travelers complaining about the road conditions north.
- Rumors that the Cragmaw goblins have a new chief.
- Halia Thornton has been asking about Wave Echo Cave (a Phalorm-era ruin) when she thinks no one's listening. Toblen has noted this.

## Quietly important

- Sister Garaele has been writing more letters than usual. Carrier pigeons in and out twice in the last week.
- Daran Edermath sharpened his old sword last evening, in plain view of the orchard road. Anyone who knows him would notice.
