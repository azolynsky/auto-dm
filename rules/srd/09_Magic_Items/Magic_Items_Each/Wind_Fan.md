### Wind Fan

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

While holding this fan, you can use an action to cast the *[[rules/srd/07_Spells/Spells_Each/Gust_of_Wind|gust of wind]]* spell (save DC 13) from it. Once used, the fan shouldn't be used again until the next dawn. Each time it is used again before then, it has a cumulative 20 percent chance of not working and tearing into useless, nonmagical tatters.
