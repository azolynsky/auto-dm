# House rules

Edit this freely as your table forms preferences. Defaults are conservative — every house rule listed has a default of **OFF** unless marked otherwise.

## Active

- **Real dice only.** All rolls — including Director "secret" rolls — go through `tools/dice.py`. The DM never says a number without a roll output to point at. (ON by default — it's the whole point of this project.)

## Suggested options to consider

- [ ] **Flanking**: two allies on opposite sides of an enemy get advantage on melee attacks. Speeds combat, encourages positioning. Default OFF (5e standard).
- [ ] **Drinking a potion as bonus action** (instead of action). Smooths combat, slight power bump.
- [ ] **Inspiration is a flat d6**: gain by clever play / good RP; spend any time before a roll to add a d6. Easier to remember than the binary version.
- [ ] **Critical hits add max + roll**: max damage of weapon dice + a normal damage roll. Less swingy than 2x dice, still feels great.
- [ ] **Resting variant**: long rest = 7 days, short rest = 8 hours (gritty realism). Default OFF — slow campaigns.
- [ ] **No multiclassing.** Simpler bookkeeping for new tables.

## Tone agreements

Things the DM and players have agreed on for the table:

- **Heroic fantasy with mortal stakes.** Not grimdark. Not comedic. The world is serious; the players are capable.
- **Complete prose, always.** No fragment sentences, no staccato shorthand narration. The DM speaks like a storyteller, not a telegraph.
- **Players drive their own decisions.** The DM never suggests how to handle a situation. The world is described; the plan is theirs.
- **Combat is play-by-play, not summary.** Each attack is narrated as it lands. Rounds are not collapsed into a paragraph.
- **Player characters are not puppeted.** The DM stops on a PC's turn and asks. Full stop.
