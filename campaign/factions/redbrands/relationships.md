# Redbrands — faction relationships

## Above them
- **Black Spider** (drow agent) — Iarno's handler. Iarno reports by sending a Redbrand runner to a dead-drop east of town on the new moon.

## Allied
- **Cragmaw goblins** — through Black Spider's network. Cragmaws kidnap, Redbrands hold ground in town. Mosk the bugbear is the practical liaison (he was a Cragmaw before being seconded here).

## Hostile / opposed
- **Phandalin civilians** — universally, though most are too scared to act
- **Sister Garaele** (Harpers) — quietly opposed; hasn't moved openly yet
- **Daran Edermath** (Harpers, retired) — known opposition; the Redbrands have left him alone because he's known to be dangerous

## Unaware of each other
- **Halia Thornton** (Zhentarim) and Iarno do not know they have overlapping interests in Wave Echo Cave.

## Watching from afar
- **The Lords' Alliance** still has a missing-persons file on Iarno Albrek from his disappearance four years ago. Nobody currently in Phandalin knows him on sight.
