# Redbrand Ruffians — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## True agenda

The Redbrands are a front. Their real purpose is to keep Phandalin small, distracted, and hostile to adventurers while **Glasstaff** (real name: **Iarno Albrek**, a former Lords' Alliance mage who turned) searches the surrounding hills for **Wave Echo Cave** and its **Forge of Spells**.

Iarno reports to the **Black Spider** — a drow agent acting on behalf of Lolth's faction in the Underdark. Black Spider wants the Forge for reasons not fully clear to Iarno himself (probably to corrupt or destroy it, though Iarno is hoping to be allowed to study it first).

## What Iarno knows
- That the Black Spider captured Gundren Rockseeker from a Cragmaw ambush and that Gundren has a map to the Cave
- That the Cragmaw goblins are working with Black Spider on contract
- That if Phandalin produces a real threat, he is to abandon the manor and rendezvous at Cragmaw Castle

## What Iarno does NOT know
- That Halia Thornton is also looking for the Cave (Zhentarim)
- That Sister Garaele is a Harper
- That Black Spider is drow (Iarno thinks he's a wizard or warlock; never seen face)

## What individual Redbrands know
Almost nothing. They're paid thugs. They know "the boss is a wizard," they know the manor cellar, they know not to ask questions. Most would flip on Glasstaff if pressed and offered amnesty.
