# Phandalin — history

## Ancient

Originally a Phalorm-era settlement (humans, dwarves, gnomes — see `world/lore.md`). Destroyed in the orcish wars ~1400 years ago. The Phalorm-era ruins underlie the modern town in places — most notably the Tresendar Manor cellars and the long-buried Wave Echo Cave somewhere east.

## Recent ancient

Rebuilt as a small human town some centuries later; was a modest farming community until the 1470s.

## The Old Phandalin destruction (1471 DR)

Orcs from Uruth Ukrypt sacked the town. Most inhabitants killed. Town abandoned. The ruins sat empty for ~17 years.

## Rebuilding (1488 DR — present)

Toblen and Trilena Stonehill arrived in 1488 with a small caravan of would-be settlers including Elmar Barthen. The town was re-founded around the inn and the trading post. Sister Garaele arrived in 1489. Daran Edermath in 1490 (retired from adventuring). Halia Thornton in 1491.

## Recent local incidents

- **~1491 DR** — Redbrand Ruffians appear, set up in the ruins of Tresendar Manor. Initially a nuisance, became extortive over the following months.
- **Late 1491** — first Cragmaw goblin raids on outlying farms.
- **Spring 1492** — Gundren Rockseeker (dwarf miner) begins talking openly in Neverwinter about a "find" near Phandalin, looking for guards to escort supplies. *(This is the campaign hook.)*
