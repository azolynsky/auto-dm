# Phandalin — geography

## Site

Sits in a shallow vale at the foot of the Sword Mountains, roughly 30 miles east of the High Road and 1 day's wagon-travel from Neverwinter via the Triboar Trail.

## Layout

The town is built around a single main road that branches into a rough square in the center. Most buildings are wood-and-stone, single-story; the inn and the manor are two-story.

- **North side**: Stonehill Inn (central), Townmaster's Hall (just north), Barthen's Provisions (north-northwest)
- **East side**: Tresendar Manor ruins (the east edge), Shrine of Luck (between the inn and the manor)
- **South side**: Lionshield Coster, Miner's Exchange, livery
- **West side**: Edermath Orchard (just outside town), the road out to Alderleaf Farm
- **Outside**: Old Owl Well sits east about a day's walk; Cragmaw caves are north-northeast

## Surroundings

- Pine and scrub forest north and east, thickening as it climbs into the foothills
- Open grazing land south, dotted with the occasional farm
- The Triboar Trail runs east-west through the town; a smaller north track leads up toward the mountains

## Climate (Mirtul / late spring)

Days warm, nights cool. Occasional thunderstorms. Roads dry; passable.

## Travel times from Phandalin

- Neverwinter: ~3 days by wagon along the Triboar Trail (a fast horseman could do it in 1.5)
- Old Owl Well: ~1 day east
- Cragmaw Hideout: ~half day north-northeast (off-trail; only locals or trackers find it)
- Cragmaw Castle: ~1 day north-northeast (deeper in)
- Wave Echo Cave: unknown to most; somewhere east
- Triboar: ~3 days east along the trail
