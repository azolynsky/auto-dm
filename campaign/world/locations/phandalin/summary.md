# Phandalin

A small frontier town, ~50 permanent residents, in the foothills of the Sword Mountains. Built on the ruins of the original Phandalin (destroyed by orcs ~1471 DR) over the last decade. Population is miners, farmers, woodsmen, and a handful of merchants serving the resurgent Triboar Trail trade.

## At a glance

- **Population**: ~50 permanent, more in mining season
- **Government**: Townmaster (Harbin Wester) — weak, advisory
- **Economy**: mining, farming, trail-trade waystation
- **Threat level**: medium and rising — Redbrand extortion unaddressed; orc and goblin raids in the surrounding hills
- **Mood**: frontier optimism rubbing against criminal anxiety

## Notable buildings (links below pull NPC folders)

- **Stonehill Inn** (central) — run by [Toblen](../../../npcs/recurring/toblen-stonehill/summary.md) and [Trilena](../../../npcs/recurring/trilena-stonehill/summary.md). De-facto town hub.
- **Lionshield Coster** — outpost of the Yartar trading company. Run by Linene Graywind.
- **Townmaster's Hall** — Harbin Wester's office.
- **Barthen's Provisions** — largest trading post. Owned by Elmar Barthen.
- **Shrine of Luck** — small shrine to Tymora, tended by [Sister Garaele](../../../npcs/recurring/sister-garaele/summary.md).
- **Edermath Orchard** — small apple orchard run by Daran Edermath.
- **Alderleaf Farm** — halfling-run, just west; Qelline Alderleaf.
- **Phandalin Miner's Exchange** — Halia Thornton runs it.
- **Tresendar Manor** — ruined manor on the east edge. Locals avoid. *(See [secrets.md](secrets.md).)*

## Active factions present

- [Redbrand Ruffians](../../../factions/redbrands/summary.md) — overt menace
- Harpers — covert (Garaele, Edermath)
- Zhentarim — suspected (see [secrets.md](secrets.md))
