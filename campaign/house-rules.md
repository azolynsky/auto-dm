# House rules

Edit this freely as your table forms preferences. Defaults are conservative — every house rule listed has a default of **OFF** unless marked otherwise.

## Active

- **Real dice only.** All rolls — including Director "secret" rolls — go through `tools/dice.py`. The DM never says a number without a roll output to point at. (ON by default — it's the whole point of this project.)
- **Named companions get death saves.** A named companion NPC/beast (Yark, Black Pearl) dropped to 0 HP falls unconscious and makes death saves like a PC instead of dying outright. Instant-death overflow rules still apply. (Ruled session 4, owlbear fight.)
- **Black Pearl is protected by the story.** Black Pearl cannot die — not by dice, not by monsters, not offscreen, not by accident. If she is ever reduced to 0 HP she is knocked out, always stabilizes, and wakes when the danger has passed. She cannot be permanently captured or lost either. This is absolute and permanent table canon; no roll overrides it. (Ruled between sessions 4 and 5.)
- **Director's gentle hand.** By table request, the "never fudge" invariant is relaxed: the Director MAY soften an outcome — redirect an enemy's attack, have foes retreat or surrender when bloodied, apply less than full damage — when the dice would push the table past fun into real distress. Rolls still go through `dice.py` and are reported honestly; the softening happens in the Director's choices, and every such call is logged in the session log as `[MERCY]` so it stays auditable. Only ever used to protect the party, never against it. (Ruled between sessions 4 and 5.)
- **Rewind for misinformed choices.** If a player's decision was based on information the chronicle failed to convey (and the character would plainly have known it), the table may rewind to that decision point. All replayed dice are rolled fresh and stand. Dice results themselves are never rewound. (Ruled session 4.)

## Suggested options to consider

- [ ] **Flanking**: two allies on opposite sides of an enemy get advantage on melee attacks. Speeds combat, encourages positioning. Default OFF (5e standard).
- [ ] **Drinking a potion as bonus action** (instead of action). Smooths combat, slight power bump.
- [ ] **Inspiration is a flat d6**: gain by clever play / good RP; spend any time before a roll to add a d6. Easier to remember than the binary version.
- [ ] **Critical hits add max + roll**: max damage of weapon dice + a normal damage roll. Less swingy than 2x dice, still feels great.
- [ ] **Resting variant**: long rest = 7 days, short rest = 8 hours (gritty realism). Default OFF — slow campaigns.
- [ ] **No multiclassing.** Simpler bookkeeping for new tables.

## Tone agreements

Things the DM and players have agreed on for the table:

- **Heroic fantasy with mortal stakes.** Not grimdark. Not comedic. The world is serious; the players are capable.
- **Complete prose, always.** No fragment sentences, no staccato shorthand narration. The DM speaks like a storyteller, not a telegraph.
- **Players drive their own decisions.** The DM never suggests how to handle a situation. The world is described; the plan is theirs.
- **Combat is play-by-play, not summary.** Each attack is narrated as it lands. Rounds are not collapsed into a paragraph.
- **Player characters are not puppeted.** The DM stops on a PC's turn and asks. Full stop.
- **Gentler stretch (active as of session 5).** Dial combat pressure down for a while: easier enemies, fewer of them, more puzzles/exploration/roleplay beats, enemies that flee or surrender rather than fight to the death. Companions and PCs should feel safe-ish even when the scene is tense. Revisit when the table asks for more danger.
