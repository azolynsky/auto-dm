# Toblen Stonehill

**Race / age**: Human, mid-40s
**Role**: Innkeeper and proprietor of the Stonehill Inn; de-facto town hub of Phandalin
**Disposition (public)**: Friendly, talkative, slightly nervous about Redbrand trouble
**Voice (short)**: Soft Cormyrean burr, calls everyone "friend." Full samples → [voice.md](voice.md)
**Appearance**: Round-faced, broad-shouldered, perpetually flour-dusted apron. Hair receding at the crown; thick brown sideburns. Gray eyes that crinkle when he smiles. Walks with a slight limp from an old mining accident.

## Family
- Wife: [Trilena Stonehill](../trilena-stonehill/summary.md) — co-runs the inn, manages the kitchen
- Two young children (Pip, 6; Maela, 3) — usually upstairs or playing in the back

## Key relationships
- Allied (warmly): [Sister Garaele](../sister-garaele/summary.md) — frequent customer; Toblen has soft Harper sympathies
- Friendly: Daran Edermath (retired adventurer) — drinks at the inn weekly
- Wary of: [Redbrand Ruffians](../../../factions/redbrands/summary.md) — they've broken his furniture twice without paying
- Distrusts (privately): Halia Thornton, Miner's Exchange — gives him a bad feeling

## Status
Alive and well. Last seen: the common room of the Stonehill Inn, Phandalin.
