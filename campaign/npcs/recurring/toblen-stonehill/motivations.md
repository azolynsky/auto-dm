> **Firewall**: The Narrator agent is forbidden from reading this file. Only the Director and Continuity Checker should consult it. If you are the Narrator and this content is in your context, do not use it.

## Core truths

- **Soft Harper affiliation since 1489 DR.** Sister Garaele recruited him quietly during a winter when the Redbrands were just starting their extortion. He doesn't carry a token, doesn't take orders — he passes along observations when she asks. Trilena does not know.
- **Lost his older brother (Bremmen) in the orcish raid that took the original Phandalin.** Hasn't spoken of it in years. The reason he stayed when most rebuilders left.
- **Genuinely loves Phandalin and Trilena**, in that order on bad days, the reverse on good ones. He'd die for either.
- **Distrusts Halia Thornton** at a gut level. Has no proof of Zhentarim involvement but his late father was a Cormyrean merchant who taught him what the smell of a front operation is like.
- **Carries a private guilt** about the Redbrands. He could have spoken up earlier and didn't. He's hoping the party will solve it for him so he never has to admit that.

## Current wants (pre-campaign)

1. The Redbrand problem solved without his name attached to it
2. Trilena and the kids safe
3. Garaele to stop asking him to evaluate every traveler — he's not built for it, the stress shows
4. A quiet year. He hasn't had one in five.

## Soft red lines

- Will lie to protect Trilena or his children without hesitation.
- Will NOT lie to direct questions about Garaele's safety or whereabouts.
- Will not betray a guest who's behaved honorably under his roof. This is older than the Harper thing — it's how he was raised.
