# Toblen Stonehill — relationships

The full network. Linked entities' summaries are the lightweight always-load; this file is the deep-dive when conversation pivots.

## Family

- **[Trilena Stonehill](../trilena-stonehill/summary.md)** — wife, 12 years married. Met in Triboar when he was passing through with a caravan. She runs the kitchen and does the books; he runs the bar and the rooms. The partnership works because they agree on almost everything. The one thing he hides from her: his Harper sympathies.
- **Pip Stonehill** (son, 6) — talkative, dark-haired, takes after Trilena.
- **Maela Stonehill** (daughter, 3) — shy, hides behind Trilena's skirt.
- **Bremmen Stonehill** (older brother, deceased ~1471 DR) — killed in the orcish raid on Old Phandalin. Toblen rarely speaks of him.

## Friends

- **[Sister Garaele](../sister-garaele/summary.md)** — closest non-family confidant. Harper connection (privately). Drinks tea, not ale, when she visits.
- **Daran Edermath** — retired adventurer, runs the orchard. Weekly drinker. Knows Toblen has "depths" but doesn't pry.
- **Elmar Barthen** (Barthen's Provisions) — friendly competitor. They send customers to each other for things the other does better.

## Wary acquaintances

- **Linene Graywind** (Lionshield Coster) — pleasant but transactional. Toblen respects her business sense, doesn't trust her allegiances.

## Distrusts

- **Halia Thornton** (Miner's Exchange) — see [motivations.md](motivations.md) for the real reason. Publicly, Toblen is polite and reserved with her. He doesn't drink her wine when she sends it.
