# Toblen Stonehill — pre-campaign tangents

Backstory minutiae that rarely comes up. Loaded only when the conversation digs deep (long evening in the inn, a PC asks about Toblen's past, a Harper contact references shared history).

## Born in Arabel, 1448 DR

Father: a Cormyrean trader who specialized in Sembian wines. Mother: died when Toblen was eight; he barely remembers her face. Father raised him and Bremmen on the road, teaching them the merchant trade by example. Toblen's gray eyes are his mother's.

## The years on the road (~1462–1471 DR)

Toblen worked his father's caravans from Arabel to Waterdeep and back. He saw most of the Sword Coast by his early twenties. He hated it — the haggling, the constant suspicion, the road food. Bremmen took to it; Toblen wanted a hearth.

## Old Phandalin and the raid (1471 DR)

The family was wintering at Old Phandalin when an orc warband from Uruth Ukrypt sacked the town. Toblen was 23. He was in the cellar of the inn where they were staying, taking inventory. Bremmen was upstairs. By the time Toblen got up the stairs, the inn was burning and his brother was dead at the door, an axe in his shoulder. Father survived but never recovered; he sold the wagon and went back to Arabel within the year. Toblen wandered north.

## Meeting Trilena (1480 DR, Triboar)

He was passing through Triboar with a different caravan when he stopped at a bakery for bread and didn't leave for three days. Trilena was 22, her father's apprentice. They were married within the season. He stayed in Triboar another seven years; they apprenticed under an innkeeper.

## Coming back to Phandalin (1488 DR)

When word came north that Phandalin was being rebuilt, Toblen knew. Trilena knew he knew, and packed without arguing. They bought the inn lot for almost nothing and built the Stonehill Inn with their own hands and a small loan from Barthen's family. Pip was born the next year.

## Garaele (1489 DR)

The next winter, Sister Garaele wandered in during a snowstorm and stayed three days waiting for it to clear. They talked. By the time she left, she knew about Bremmen and the raid; he knew she was a Harper. The arrangement formed naturally — he never agreed to anything formal, but when she asks, he tells.

## Why "Stonehill"

It's not the family name — the family name is *Stonehill of Arabel*, but he dropped the geography when he came north. The inn was named first; the name stuck and he uses it for the family now. Trilena thinks it's funny; he thinks it's appropriate. Old Phandalin had a stone hill outside the south gate where Bremmen used to climb to watch the road.
