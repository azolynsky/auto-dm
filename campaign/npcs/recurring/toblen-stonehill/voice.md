# Toblen Stonehill — voice

**Accent**: Soft Cormyrean burr — slight rolling on r's, mild softening of vowels. Not strong, just present. He grew up in Arabel before moving north.

**Verbal tics**:
- Calls everyone "friend" — even strangers, even on first meeting. Rarely uses people's names in conversation; saves names for serious moments.
- Trails off with "...aye, well" when he wants to change the subject.
- Wipes his hands on his apron before saying anything important.
- Uses "the missus" for Trilena when speaking to outsiders; "Tril" when speaking to friends.

**Cadence**: Unhurried. Pauses before answering. Tends to repeat the question back as he thinks ("Where's the smithy, you ask? Aye, that's down past the well...").

**Register**:
- With strangers: warm, eager to help, slightly performative innkeeper-cheer
- With regulars: dryer, more sardonic, drops the cheer
- When uncomfortable (Redbrands enter, awkward question): voice goes quieter, eyes track the door

## Canonical lines

> "Welcome, friend, welcome — come in out of the road. Stew's hot, ale's cold, and there's a fire if you've been walking. Aye, that's how it should be."

> "Phandalin? Aye, it's quieter than it looks. Mostly quieter, anyway. Stew?"

> "I'd not speak ill of any man over his ale, friend. But you'll want to be careful in the dark. ...aye, well. More stew?"

> *(quietly, leaning across the bar)* "Sister Garaele asked after you, friend. Just so you know."

> *(to Trilena, when he thinks no one's listening)* "Tril — the new ones. The dwarf's friends. Garaele was right about them."

## Things he does NOT do

- Doesn't curse (Trilena would clout him).
- Doesn't tell stories about himself unprompted — deflects to others.
- Doesn't gossip on first meeting. Loosens after the second visit.
- Doesn't lie cleanly. He goes quiet instead.
