# Sildar Hallwinter — GM EYES ONLY

> **Firewall**: Narrator must not read this file.

## Core truths

- **His primary mission is the Forge of Spells**, not Gundren personally. The Lords' Alliance wants the forge secured or destroyed before any hostile faction can use it. Gundren's rescue is a means to that end — though Sildar does genuinely like Gundren and wants him safe.
- **He knows more about the Black Spider than he's told the party.** Alliance intelligence suggests the Spider has been active for years, recruiting goblinoid tribes and suborning merchants along the Sword Coast. He hasn't shared this because he's still assessing whether the party can be trusted with the full picture.
- **He has a contact in Phandalin**: Iarno Albrek, a fellow Alliance agent sent ahead six weeks ago to establish contacts. Iarno has gone silent. Sildar is quietly worried. *(GM note: Iarno IS Glasstaff, leader of the Redbrands — he turned.)*
- **He will not tell the party about Iarno unless they discover it themselves or he has a very strong reason.** Loyalty to a brother agent, plus embarrassment that the Alliance has a bad actor in its ranks.

## Current wants
1. Locate Cragmaw Castle and rescue Gundren
2. Find out what happened to Iarno Albrek
3. Secure Wave Echo Cave before the Black Spider
4. Keep the party on-side — they're the best asset he has right now

## Soft red lines
- Will not reveal Iarno's name until forced to.
- Will not betray the party to Alliance interests — his personal code won't allow it.
- Will share Alliance resources (contacts, letters of credit, maps) if the party proves trustworthy.
