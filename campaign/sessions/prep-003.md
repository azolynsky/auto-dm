Key areas: main gate (barred), collapsed south-east wall (Yark/Grib's supply route), great hall (Grol's throne), Grol's private chamber (Gundren + map + Grol + his wolf Snarl), owlbear tower, shrine room (grick), barracks. Garrison alerted but not on high alert — they think the search parties are handling it. If alarm is raised, resistance organizes by room; otherwise rooms can be taken piecemeal.
