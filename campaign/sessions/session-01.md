# Session 01 — The Goblin Trail

**Real date**: 2026-05-17
**In-game date**: 1st of Mirtul, 1492 DR — morning
**Starting location**: The Stonehill Inn, common room, Phandalin

---

## Log

*(Running log — updated turn by turn)*

### Opening
Party: Relthus (Wood Elf Ranger, Alex) and Mira (Human Fighter, Olive). Session begins at the Stonehill Inn, Phandalin, common room, morning.

### At the Stonehill Inn (morning)
- Met Toblen Stonehill; ate breakfast and reported the Triboar Trail ambush site.
- Toblen warned of Redbrand extortion in town. Mentioned a kidnapped miner who came back.
- Party decided to track Gundren rather than settle in Phandalin.

### Triboar Trail — Ambush Site
- Relthus tracked goblin trail (Survival +4 advantage, rolled 12, success margin 2). Trail leads north into a ravine.
- Mira searched the site (Investigation, rolled 17, success margin 5). Found Gundren's hidden note: "The Spider has ears in Neverwinter. Don't write this down."
- Cragmaw Hideout located: ravine, stream-mouth cave, north-northeast.

### Cragmaw Hideout — Exterior
- Relthus observed the wolf: captive, not allied with goblins. Signaled Mira.
- Mira attempted to flank (Stealth 9, fail) — wolf smelled her, goblins alerted.
- Combat: Relthus shot GoblinArcher (dead, 10 damage). GoblinBlade hit Mira (7 damage). Mira missed. Relthus killed GoblinBackup (8 damage). GoblinBlade hit Mira again (3 damage, 2 HP). Mira Second Wind (2 HP back, 4 HP). Mira's rapier left GoblinBlade at 1 HP.
- Party chose to capture GoblinBlade (Yark) for interrogation rather than finish it.
- Short rest (1 hour): Mira spent hit die, rolled 10, recovered to full 12/12.
- Wolf freed. It left without looking back.

### Cragmaw Hideout — Interior
- Yark attempted to bluff ledge goblins (Deception 1, catastrophic fail). Rocks dropped.
- Relthus shot and missed (9, fail). Took rockslide damage (DEX save 9, fail) — 4 damage, 8/12 HP.
- Retreated. Yark revealed stream passage, Klarg's wolf (Ripper), one goblin guard near Sildar.
- Stream passage: both PCs moved silently (Relthus 22, Mira 18 — both success).
- Relthus shot goblin guard from hiding with advantage (23 to hit, 8 damage — dead).
- Sildar freed. He confirmed: Black Spider is likely a drow, wants Wave Echo Cave and its Forge of Spells. Gundren taken to Cragmaw Castle with the map. He is a Lords' Alliance agent.
- Yark led them out via stream passage, then released. Thanked.

### Return to Phandalin (midday)
- Arrived at Stonehill Inn. Toblen asked no questions, brought stew.
- Sildar: "Gundren is at Cragmaw Castle. That's the next problem. But it can wait until tomorrow."

## XP Awarded

- Sildar rescued (significant objective): 200 XP total, 100 XP per PC
- **Each PC: +200 XP (now 200/300 toward level 2)**

- Mira: 12/12 HP, hit die spent, Second Wind available, 200 XP
