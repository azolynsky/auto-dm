# Session 04 — The Owlbear's Toll

**Real date**: 2026-07-22
**In-game**: 3rd–4th of Mirtul, early night → ~1.5h before dawn
**Location at start**: Cragmaw Castle — kitchen (secured)
**Location at end**: Cragmaw Castle — north corridor at Grol's tower door (CLIFFHANGER)

## Recap

The party scouted the great hall (map tube in play between Grol and the messenger), looted the storeroom, and tried to sneak past the owlbear — and failed. The fight nearly ended the campaign: Relthus dropped three times (one table rewind over unclear two-attack info; his replayed choice still went down), Pearl fell, and Yark stabilized them both while Mira held the line alone and finally showed the beaten owlbear mercy, letting it retreat (12/59, alive in its den). The castle found the dead cooks and went on alert but feared the owlbear passage too much to search it. After a night watch and short rest, the party outsmarted the grick in the shrine with two thrown coins — splitting the party across it — and Relthus and Mira reached Grol's tower door: one hobgoblin sentry outside, and inside, Grol and the messenger closing a deal that now includes selling Gundren himself. Both PCs hit 975 XP — level 3 pending.
