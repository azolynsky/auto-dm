# Campaign so far

## Sessions 1–2 (1st–3rd Mirtul, 1492 DR)

Relthus and Mira escorted Gundren Rockseeker's supplies to Phandalin, learned he'd been ambushed by Cragmaw goblins, and rescued Sildar Hallwinter (Lords' Alliance agent) from the Cragmaw Hideout. Gundren and his map to Wave Echo Cave were taken to Cragmaw Castle on the Black Spider's orders.

In Session 2 the party recruited Yark — the goblin they'd spared — as guide to Cragmaw Castle (he'd been exiled; Klarg blames him for the hideout losses). En route: killed two of Klarg's hunters at night camp; won over a Cragmaw patrol's mistreated wolf (now Mira's companion, **Black Pearl**); and at dusk on day 3, half a mile from the castle, wiped out a 3-goblin search party — two dead, one prisoner (**Grib**, who works inside the castle). Both PCs hit **level 2 (not yet applied)**.

## Session 3 (3rd Mirtul, night)

Level 2 applied (Relthus: Cure Wounds + Ensnaring Strike; Mira: Action Surge). Grib talked under threat — King Grol holds Gundren and the map in his rear tower; a drow messenger is there NOW buying the map; ~2 dozen garrison; owlbear tower; SE collapsed wall is the way in — then Relthus executed him. The party sniped the SE wall sentry, entered via the supply corridor, and silently killed both kitchen cooks. No alarm. Both PCs at 400/900 XP.

## Session 4 (night of 3rd–4th Mirtul)

The owlbear caught the party sneaking past: Relthus dropped three times (one table rewind logged), Pearl fell, Yark stabilized both, and Mira fought the beast alone before showing it mercy — it lives, wounded, in its den. The castle found the dead cooks and alerted, but fears the owlbear passage. After a short rest the party coin-tricked the grick and split across the shrine: Relthus + Mira reached Grol's tower door. Overheard inside: the messenger's final offer includes taking Gundren (alive, defiant). Both PCs 975 XP — **level 3 pending**.

## Session 5 (4th Mirtul — with guest player Mitch as Gundren)

Level 3 applied (Relthus: Hunter/Colossus Slayer + Hunter's Mark; Mira: Champion). The party spared the tower sentry (mercy later repaid), solved the puzzle-lock on Grol's door via Gundren's shouted hints, and stormed the chamber. Mira won over Grol's wolf **Snarl** mid-fight; the "drow" courier was unmasked as a **doppelganger (Vyerith)** and fled out the window with the map; Gundren turned Grol against the Spider's buyer with one shout. Bloodied and disarmed, **Grol traded the ambush route for his life and castle** (truce holds). The party beat the thief to the plank bridge, dropped a rockslide on it, and Relthus's arrow finished the capture. **Gundren rescued, map recovered, Vyerith taken alive.** Long rest through the day. Both PCs at 1375 XP. New house rules: Black Pearl absolute plot armor; Director's gentle hand (`[MERCY]`, used once on a crit vs Gundren).
