# The Dire Wraiths

Shapeshifting sorcerous aliens who drain life and wear their victims'
faces. A horror-flavored roster: **Vile Darkness** (elite flying terror,
functionally a dark twin of a hive queen), **Deathwing Spellcasters**
(flying goon controllers who entangle and reposition), the **Dire Wraiths**
themselves (goon drainers who leech HP and dominate the dying), and
**Hellhounds** (phase-charging goon beasts).

The fear/harried mechanics stack viciously — run them as an escalating
dread engine, and lean on their shapeshifting for paranoia scenes out of
combat (who's really who?).

Statblocks: `stats.json` (level 4; the "4✸ (or 14 HP)" thresholds mean you
can run those units either as hit boxes or as an HP pool — pick one per
fight and stay consistent).
