# The NIMROD Virus

A self-replicating Sentinel intelligence from a ruined future — the pitch
rates Nimrod <4>, the heaviest single threat on the menu. Runs as a
**"Double Elite"** boss (46 HP, flying, acts on initiative counts 7/5/3
each round) that **infects** two targets per turn, converting them into
**Huntsmen** (1-HP brawler pack units it also commands and consumes as
ammunition: revive them, detonate them, sacrifice them to ignore attacks).

This is a raid boss: the fight is about managing the growing pack and the
action-economy pressure of a boss that acts three times a round. Terrain
and focus-fire matter; so does deciding whether to hit the boss or thin the
pack.

Statblocks: `stats.json` (level 4).
