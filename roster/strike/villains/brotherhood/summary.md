# The Brotherhood (of Evil Mutants)

Mystique's cell of mutant radicals: Mystique herself (elite shapeshifting
infiltrator), the Blob (immovable brute), Toad (sticky skirmisher),
Avalanche (terrain-wrecking controller), and Pyro (area-denial blaster).
The pitch doc rates a full-team fight around Mystique <1.75> + Blob <1.25>
+ the rest at standard weight, with **Magneto <4>** available as an
escalation nobody should meet early.

Each member is an HP-tracked named villain, not a faceless goon squad — mix
and match subsets, or run one of them solo with hired muscle underneath.

Statblocks: `stats.json` (level 4; Magneto not statted — build him when the
campaign earns it).
