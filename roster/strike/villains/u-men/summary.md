# The U-Men

Anti-mutant zealots in powered exosuits who harvest mutant organs to graft
mutant abilities onto themselves. Fielded as a three-unit strike force:
black-armored **Elites** (stooge leaders who buff and coordinate),
red-armored **Hammers** (goon brutes with hydraulic grapples), and
green-armored **Rangers** (goon snipers with flechette cannons).

Use them for raids, abductions, and lab-defense fights. They fight like a
disciplined unit: Elites hang back granting saves, Hammers grab and pin,
Rangers punish movement.

Statblocks: `stats.json` (level 4; quantities scale by party size).
