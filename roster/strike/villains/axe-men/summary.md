# The A.X.E.Men

An elite paramilitary axe-wielding successor force to the U-Men — same
harvest-the-mutants ideology, better hardware. Tougher across the board:
even the rank-and-file are stooge *strikers* with neurodisruptor axes and
jump jets, screened by leaders and sniper support.

Use them when the table has learned to chew through U-Men: fewer bodies,
higher threat. They pair well with a named mastermind as the "muscle" wave.

Statblocks: `stats.json` (level 4; quantities scale by party size).
