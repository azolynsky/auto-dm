# The Brood

Insectoid parasitic aliens that implant embryos in living hosts. A hive
fight: the **Brood Queen** (elite horror, flying, aura of fear) surrounded
by **Brood Warriors** (stooge grapplers with dragging tentacles). The pitch
rates the Queen <2> with warriors <0.5> to fill.

Play them as terror: the Queen's aura and panic effects punish clustering,
the warriors grab and drag heroes away from each other. Infestation and
implantation make great non-combat stakes (skill scenes, Team Conflict)
between fights.

Statblocks: `stats.json` (level 4).
